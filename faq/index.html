FAQs

What’s Included in a Health Checkup Package?

A health checkup package includes tests to check your overall health, like blood tests, heart checks, and more. It’s designed to find any hidden health issues.

Is There a Special Package for Women’s Health?

Yes, we offer packages specifically for women’s health, covering important tests like hormone levels, breast health, and more.

How Often Should I Do a Full Body Checkup?

It’s good to do a full body checkup once a year to catch any health problems early.

Do You Provide Home Sample Collection?

Yes, we offer home sample collection so you can get tested without leaving your home.

How Much Does a Full Body Checkup Cost in Patna?

The cost depends on the package you choose. Contact us to know the exact price for your preferred package.

Do I Need to Fast Before My Health Checkup?

Some tests require fasting for accurate results. Our team will inform you if fasting is needed before your checkup.
